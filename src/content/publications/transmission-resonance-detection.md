---
title: "Real-time detection of linear and nonlinear resonance modes in automatic transmission driveline: A novel algorithm for enhanced reliability"
publisher: "Journal of Mechanical Science and Technology"
pubDate: 2025-08-01
link: "https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12206-025-0701-8"
---

This paper proposes an algorithm for the real-time detection of linear and nonlinear resonance modes in rotating driveline shafts using an onboard transmission control module (TCM). By utilizing Order-based Fast Fourier Transforms (FFT) on vibration signals from integrated speed sensors, the algorithm enables the detection of natural frequencies across wide operating ranges, providing a crucial tool for mitigating fatigue failures caused by cyclic loading in medium-duty and heavy-duty vehicles.
