---
title: "A Genetic Algorithm for Optimizing Fantasy Football Trades with Playoff Biasing"
publisher: "Arxiv"
pubDate: 2025-11-05
link: "https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.17535"
---

This research introduces a genetic algorithm designed to automate the discovery of mutually beneficial fantasy football trades, specifically biasing toward late-season playoff performance. By utilizing custom mutations and team-specific elitism, the model evolves trades that improve projected point totals for both partners while maintaining a level of perceived fairness necessary for successful negotiation.
