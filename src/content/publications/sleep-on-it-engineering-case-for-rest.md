Sleep is often viewed as a luxury or a byproduct of a productive day, but the research suggests it is actually a foundational requirement for cognitive performance. This post explores the engineering case for rest, focusing on how sleep facilitates memory consolidation, creative problem-solving, and emotional regulation, and why skipping it is one of the most expensive technical debts you can accrue.
