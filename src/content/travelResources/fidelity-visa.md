The Fidelity® Visa Signature® Card is my default card for international travel. Simple rewards, no foreign transaction fees, and broad acceptance worldwide.

### Why I use it

- **No Foreign Transaction Fees:** Many credit cards quietly charge a 3% fee on every international purchase. Spend $4,000 abroad and that is an extra $120 gone for nothing. The Fidelity card charges 0%.
- **Flat 2% Cash Back:** Everything earns 2% cash back with no categories to track or activate. Rewards can be automatically deposited into a Fidelity brokerage, IRA, or cash management account.
- **Global Entry / TSA PreCheck Credit:** The card reimburses the application fee for Global Entry or TSA PreCheck once every four years. Technically this comes as 10,000 Reward Points, equal to $100 when redeemed into a qualifying Fidelity account.
- **Visa Acceptance Worldwide:** Visa is accepted almost everywhere internationally, including many places where Amex is unreliable or not accepted at all.
- **Pairs Perfectly with the Fidelity Debit Card:** The setup works especially well alongside the Fidelity Cash Management Account debit card. Use the credit card for purchases and the debit card for fee-free ATM withdrawals abroad.

### A Few Things to Know
- The full 2% value requires redeeming rewards into an eligible Fidelity account.
- The card is best for people who value simplicity over travel points optimization.
- Approval generally requires good credit.

### Getting Started
You can apply directly through Fidelity online. If you already use Fidelity for investing or banking, it integrates seamlessly into the rest of the ecosystem.
