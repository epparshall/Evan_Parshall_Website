The Fidelity® Visa Signature® Card is one of the best tools for international travelers, especially if you already use Fidelity for your investments.

### Why I use it:
- **No Foreign Transaction Fees:** Most cards charge 3% on every purchase abroad. This card charges 0%.
- **2% Cash Back:** Everything you buy earns 2% back, which can be deposited directly into your Fidelity brokerage or retirement account.
- **Visa Acceptance:** Unlike Amex or Discover, Visa is accepted almost everywhere in the world.

### How to apply:
You can apply through the [Fidelity website](https://www.fidelity.com/spend-save/visa-signature-card). It usually requires a decent credit score, but the long-term savings on travel fees make it well worth it.
