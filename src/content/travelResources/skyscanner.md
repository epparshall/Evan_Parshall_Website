---
title: "Skyscanner"
description: "The best tool for finding cheap, flexible flights across different airlines and regions."
---

When I'm planning a trip, I almost always start with Skyscanner. Unlike Google Flights, it often picks up smaller budget airlines and third-party booking sites that can save you a lot of money.

### Top Tips:
- **Use the 'Everywhere' Search:** If you just want to go *somewhere*, search from your home airport to 'Everywhere' to see the cheapest destinations.
- **Monthly View:** Instead of picking a specific date, look at the whole month to find the cheapest days to fly.
